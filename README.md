# Debt_Finance

This repository consolidates applied Debt Finance & Credit Risk analytics work.
It is designed as a technical learning portfolio covering credit-decision logic, portfolio segmentation, early-warning indicators, KPI engineering, and scenario/sensitivity analysis.

# Credit Decisioning  
The focus is on:
• Understanding how lending decisions are made  
• Applying quantitative methods to real-world credit problems  
• Demonstrating business logic, segmentation, PD drivers, and portfolio monitoring  
• Communicating insights clearly for risk, product, and credit-policy stakeholders  

# Risk Analyst
The focus is on:
• Monitoring MoM and rolling-window trends  
• Identifying deterioration signals  
• Assessing concentration risk  
• Producing concise, insight-driven commentary  

# Technical Competency

Python: pandas, numpy, matplotlib (selected)
SQL: joins, CTEs, filters, aggregations
KPI engineering, MoM/MoM trend decomposition
Heatmaps & visual diagnostics

# Risk Narrative & Stakeholder Reporting
Structured commentary: what moved, why it moved, what it means
Clear articulation of drivers and risk implications
Insight-focused, business-oriented reporting
