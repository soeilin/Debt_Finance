# Debt Analytics Portfolio  
A curated collection of practical, lightweight credit-risk and portfolio-analysis projects, aligned with entry-level roles in Credit Decisioning, Risk Analytics, and Retail/SME Portfolio Management.  
Each project reflects core analytical thinking, quantitative fundamentals, and data-driven credit logic — without overstating model-development expertise.

The focus is on:
• Understanding how lending decisions are made  
• Applying quantitative methods to real-world credit problems  
• Demonstrating business logic, segmentation, PD drivers, and portfolio monitoring  
• Communicating insights clearly for risk, product, and credit-policy stakeholders  

This portfolio intentionally avoids complex modelling (e.g., advanced scorecard development, IFRS9 calibration) and instead highlights the foundational skillset required for analyst-level credit and risk roles.

---

## Project Overview

### 1. Credit Strategy Lite (Rule-based Decisioning Prototype)
A simplified credit-strategy framework illustrating how lending rules, risk flags, and approval/decline logic interact within an automated decision engine.  
Includes cut-off rules, affordability checks, risk triggers, and decision outcomes.

### 2. Portfolio KPI Monitoring (MoM / Risk Trends)
A basic monthly risk dashboard showing delinquency trends, bad-rate movement, approval rate, utilisation, and simple cohort performance.  
Focus on interpretation and operational insight rather than modelling complexity.

### 3. K-Means Segmentation (Behavioural / Risk Clustering)
A refreshed segmentation exercise demonstrating how behavioural variables can be used to group customers with similar lending or repayment characteristics.  
Highlights the translation of clusters into credit-strategy actions.

### 4. PD Model Lite (Logistic Regression)
A baseline logistic-regression model predicting probability of default using a small set of clean features.  
Emphasis on coefficient interpretation, ROC/AUC, PD ranking, and business impact — not on scorecard methodology.

### 5. Regression (Elasticity / Drivers Analysis)
A lightweight regression study to explain key portfolio metrics or commercial outcomes (e.g., spend, utilisation, revenue drivers).  
Shows ability to translate statistical output into actionable business insights.
