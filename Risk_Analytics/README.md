# Risk Analytics
This directory contains applied analytical work aligned to the responsibilities of a Risk Analyst supporting retail-credit portfolios.
The focus is on diagnosing movements in portfolio performance, identifying emerging risks, and presenting insights in a structured, decision-ready format.

# 1. Portfolio KPI & Trend Analysis (MoM / Risk Trends)
Build a monitoring pack capturing portfolio health over time and identifying early behavioural shifts.

**Key Outputs**

• Approval / utilisation / arrears / bad-rate KPIs  
• Month-on-month and rolling-window trend views  
• Variance decomposition and risk attribution  
• Segmentation-based deterioration analysis  
• Structured commentary: what moved / why / implications  

**Risk Concepts Illustrated**

• Early-warning signals  
• Driver-based risk interpretation  
• Portfolio stability assessment  
• Risk appetite alignment at a basic level  


# 2. Portfolio Concentration Heatmap
Detect structural concentration within exposures or risk outcomes.

**Key Outputs**

• Exposure distribution by geography, product, or borrower attributes 
• Heatmap to identify clusters of elevated utilisation or delinquency 
• Interpretation of concentration risk and potential implications 

**Risk Concepts Illustrated**

• Concentration risk frameworks  
• Segmentation & pocket-level deterioration  
• Exposure distribution dynamics  

# 3. Technical Capabilities Demonstrated

**Tools - Python**

• Data cleaning & preparation (pandas) 
• KPI construction and transformation 
• Trend & variance analysis 
• Heatmap visualisation (matplotlib / seaborn if used) 

**Analytics Discipline**

• Consistent metric definitions 
• Driver-based decomposition 
• Clear, logical interpretation aligned with risk-management expectations
